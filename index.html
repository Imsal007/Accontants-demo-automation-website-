Questions

Straight answers

What does it cost?

A fixed monthly fee agreed before we start, based on your type of business and how much bookkeeping you need. No hourly billing and no surprise invoices in January.

What happens after I enquire?

You get an instant reply with a booking link. After a free 20-minute call we send your engagement letter, a secure ID check and a document checklist.

Does automation replace the personal touch?

No. The system handles the reminders, so our time goes into the conversations that matter: planning, advice and answering your questions. You always reach a real person.

How is my ID checked?

Through a specialist identity verification provider. You complete it on your phone in a few minutes, and we review the result before we start.

Is my data safe?

Documents are shared through secure links, not loose email attachments. We follow UK GDPR, and demo emails are sent only with your consent.

Can I switch from my current accountant?

Yes. We send the professional clearance letter and collect your records, so you do not have to explain your history twice.
