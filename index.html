Questions

Straight answers

What does it cost?

Fixed monthly fees agreed before we start, based on your type of client and how much bookkeeping you need. No hourly billing and no surprise invoices in January.

What happens after I enquire?

You receive an instant reply with a booking link. After a free 20-minute call we send an engagement letter and a secure ID check. Most clients are fully onboarded within two working days.

Does automation replace the personal touch?

No. The system handles the chasing, so the time goes into the conversations that matter: planning, advice and answering your questions. You always reach a real person.

Is my data safe?

Documents are shared through secure links, never as loose email attachments. We follow UK GDPR, and demo emails are sent only with your consent.

Can I switch from my current accountant?

Yes. We send the professional clearance letter and collect your records, so you do not have to explain your history twice.
